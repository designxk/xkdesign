About Changsha Xiaokai Industrial Design Co., Ltd.

Changsha Xiaokai Industrial Design Co., Ltd. is a professional OEM/ODM manufacturer specializing in smart cabinets, AI intelligent display cabinets, refrigerated fresh cabinets, energy storage enclosures, and precision sheet metal structure design.

With years of experience in industrial design and manufacturing, we provide complete solutions from concept design, structural engineering, prototyping to mass production, serving global clients in retail, logistics, healthcare, and new energy industries.
